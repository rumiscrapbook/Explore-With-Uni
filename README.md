🦄 Uni's Little Adventure

Welcome to Uni's Little Adventure! ✨

Meet Uni, a magical unicorn who's just moved into a colourful little town. 
Join her as she explores her new surroundings and begins her journey!

🌷 About the Project

This is a fun little interactive project made with Scratch. It features a unicorn character, 
a colourful city backdrop, dialogue, and recorded audio.

✨ Features
🦄 An adorable unicorn character named Uni
🏙️ A colourful town setting
💬 Interactive character dialogue
🎙️ Recorded audio

🎮 How to Play
Open the project in Scratch.
Press the Space key.
Listen to Uni's introduction!
